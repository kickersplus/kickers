Business

Process

1. Purchase Kicker Pair

No registration fee. Instead, become a "DEALER" by purchasing a KICKER PAIR, 2 pairs of any shoes.

2. Account Activation

Buying the Kicker Pair grants you an official dealer account, a lifetime 25% shoes discount and free training.

3. Build a Binary Netwotk

The networking framework works in a binary system and required to build and manage two sales teams. Group 1 Sales Team (left) and Group 2 Sales Team (right).

4. Sponsor and invite to be a dealer

Income (25%)

1. Retail profit (Direct Selling)

Income: Net 25% profit. Dealers buy products at a 25% wholesale discount and sell the to customer at the Standard Retail Price. No monthly sales quotas to maintain discount.

2. Online commission

The system will provide the dealer a unique online store.

3. Sales commission (Direct referral)

Income: P500 per direct referral. Every time you personally sponsor or sell a Kicker Pair to a new dealer, you receive a direct cash incentive.

4. Group Sales override commission

Income: P1,500 per matched pair (up to a maximum of P15,000 per day or P300,000 per month). The core of networking income stream. Whenever the system detects one new Kicker Pair Sale in Group 1 and another in Group 2, it creates a "sales match" or "pair". It doesnt matter if you personally invited them or they were brought by downline team.

5. Residual Rebates (6%)

3% - 1st Level

2% - 2nd Level

1% - 3rd Level

6% total split across 3 tiers. You earned a percentage of residual income based on the repeat, non Kicker Pair purchased by your downline network up to 3rd level.
